## Limits of this run

- The development data holds no supplier bill, settlement, credit note or case, so step 3 proves those tables restore, not their rows. The Phase 2 exit pilot (`apps/api/test/pilot/phase2-exit.api.spec.ts`) proves their behaviour.
- The journey in step 7 is the Phase 1 one. Add a Phase 2 read (a delivery, a case) when the drill moves to staging data.
